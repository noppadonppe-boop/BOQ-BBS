# Inter-bar model-bounds review — v0.86

The member editor has a `ตรวจเหล็กทับกัน` tab. A dedicated Worker compares circular bounds around the model centrelines of different physical bar pieces, reports Mark/pose identities, estimated surface-gap bounds and nearby model points, and links a Mark to the existing3D inspector. The check is local and disposable; changing member data clears results, and cancellation terminates the Worker without returning a healthy zero.

The check uses the existing `barPaths` transforms and sampled rounded/arc/helix geometry, outside diameter where supplied and nominal diameter otherwise. Missing outside diameters are disclosed. Curve sagitta and numeric error bounds widen the reported interval. Negative circular-bound gaps are review candidates, including endpoint-cap candidates; they are not a certified intersection of the actual flat-ended manufactured solids. Exact straight-bar tangency is not reported as overlap at the numeric resolution1e-5mm, which is not an engineering acceptance tolerance.

Scope is one member's physical bar pieces. Occurrence quantities/Zone/Floor copies are not placed on top of each other. Same-piece closure/self-interference, BS/ACI minimum clear spacing, adjacent members and full physical fabrication review are excluded. Existing code33/51 closure holds and engineering/source/issue gates remain unchanged. No calculation engine, quantities, persistent schema or approval policy changed.

Current bounds:4000pieces,50000segments,1000000broad-phase visits,1000stored candidate pairs; the UI shows100rows. Exceeded bounds return Incomplete with the available evidence, not a completed no-issue result. Invalid/empty geometry and a missing target solid cannot yield a healthy empty result. A spread-based sweep axis avoids a long-axis quadratic queue for spaced bars. The UI does not yet have a piece-subset selector; large datasets require further tooling rather than editing real geometry to fit these limits.
